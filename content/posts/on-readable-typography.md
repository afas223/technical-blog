---
title: On Readable Typography
date: 2026-03-02
description: Serif body copy and generous line height go a long way.
---

Long-form posts ask for calm typography: a comfortable measure, line height around **1.75**, and headings that step down in size instead of shouting.

If everything is bold, nothing is. Pick one accent color for links and reuse it—visitors learn the pattern in seconds.
