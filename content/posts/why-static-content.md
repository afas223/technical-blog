---
title: Why Static Content Is Enough for Most Blogs
date: 2026-04-18
description: Fast pages, simple deploys, fewer moving parts.
---

Most personal sites do not need a request-time database. **Markdown in Git** plus a static build gives you versioning, reviews, and backups for free.

When you *do* need something dynamic—comments, newsletters, forms—you can bolt on a hosted service later without rewriting the whole site.

The trick is to keep the reading experience boringly fast: small HTML, readable type, and links that look like links.
